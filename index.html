Building Healthier Lives. Creating Brighter Futures.

Hopebridge Health & Education Foundation is an initiative committed to helping vulnerable children and underserved communities access education, health support and opportunities for a better future.

Explore Our Programmes Make a Difference

About Hopebridge

Hope, opportunity and care for every community.

We believe that every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow and live a healthy life, regardless of their family's financial circumstances.

Hopebridge is being developed to connect individuals, volunteers, partners and donors with practical community initiatives, with an initial focus on vulnerable children and rural communities in Uganda.

Our Programmes

Our planned areas of work respond to essential community needs.

📚

Education Support

Help vulnerable children access essential school requirements, including scholastic materials and other learning necessities.

🍲

School Meals

Develop initiatives to support school meals and help children learn in a more supportive environment.

🩺

Health and Wellbeing

Promote health awareness, preventive health education and connections to appropriate community health services.

🤝

Community Development

Work towards partnerships that strengthen families, encourage volunteering and create opportunities for children.

Our Priority Project

Supporting vulnerable children in rural Uganda.

School Requirements for Children

Our priority project aims to help children from financially vulnerable households obtain the essential requirements they need to attend school and participate in learning.

We aim to work with community representatives, schools, potential donors and local partners to identify needs and direct support responsibly.

School Meals Initiative

We aim to explore partnerships that can help schools provide meals for children who face food insecurity, supporting their wellbeing and participation in education.
